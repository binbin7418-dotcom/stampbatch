What it does

  • Text or logo marks — a copyright line in white or black, or your own logo image with transparency
  • Nine-grid positioning, plus opacity and size controls that scale from thumbnail to master file
  • Tiled mode — repeat the mark across the full frame for proof and preview images
  • Platform presets for Etsy and Instagram that pre-tune mark size and margin (presets never change your photo's dimensions)
  • A re-check report with every batch — each output re-opened, size verified, watermark area pixel-compared
  • Originals never touched — results are written to a separate folder

What it never does

StampBatch only watermarks. It does not resize, crop, rename or convert your photos, so it can't silently degrade or re-frame them — output dimensions are always identical to the input, and the re-check proves it. And honestly: a watermark is a deterrent that raises the cost of stealing your images, not copy protection. Anyone who promises more is guessing.
